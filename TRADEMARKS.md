## Attribution

## Allowed

- Truthfully stating that your project is based on or forked from BitCraft Claim Monitor
- Keeping the original repository attribution intact
- Referencing the original project in changelogs, docs, or acknowledgements

- Implying that your fork is the official BitCraft Claim Monitor

## Recommended Fork Wording

If you run a modified public deployment, use wording similar to:
